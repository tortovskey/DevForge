DevForge 1.0.0 uses CPython/Pyodide, sql.js/SQLite, PGlite/PostgreSQL, Fengari/Lua, Monaco, React, React Router, Lucide, FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, HTTPX, and dependencies. Native windows use pywebview, pythonnet and WebView2 bindings. Original license files are available here and in bundled packages. Exact dependency versions are recorded in package-lock.json and requirements files. Microsoft WebView2 Runtime is installed separately; Setup includes Microsoft's signed bootstrapper for computers without the Runtime. No AI model weights or JetBrains components are distributed.

DevForge also includes binji/wasm-clang (Apache 2.0, LLVM and sysroot notices in wasm-clang-LICENSE files) and the CPython sqlite3 Pyodide module. Original toolchain bytes are pinned and verified by SHA256. DevForge's MIT license applies to its own code; third-party components retain their original licenses.
